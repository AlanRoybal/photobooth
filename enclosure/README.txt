HackUTD Zero Day photobooth case, concept v1 (Bambu Lab X1C)

Assembled size: 244 W x 318 D x 356 H mm, plus the optional 175 mm paper tunnel behind it.

FILES
Open each .3mf in Bambu Studio. Each one is already rotated for printing and fits the X1C plate.
Colours are already assigned in each 3MF by AMS slot. Load your AMS like this:
  Slot 1: black (all case parts)
  Slot 2: white (title letters)
  Slot 3: magenta (title accents, screen frame, camera ring)
  Slot 4: cyan (title accents, camera arrow, photo slot stripe)
If your AMS is loaded differently, change the filament for each part in Bambu Studio's object list. Every part's name ends in its colour (e.g. sign_magenta); parts with no colour in the name are black.
The .stl files are the same parts if you need them separately.

01_tower                 screen window, camera hole, Pi bay. Prints front face down.
02_tower_rear_panel      Pi 5 screws onto its 4 posts. Closes the back of the tower.
03_screen_retainer_bars  2 bars that clamp the touchscreen against the front.
04_base_front            photo slot and printer bay. Bolts to 05 and holds the tower. Turn supports ON (build plate only) for the ink door window.
05_base_rear             back of the bay, open behind the printer for the paper.
06_lid                   covers the printer; window shows the SELPHY's own screen and buttons.
07_ink_door_cover        press-fit cover over the side window used to change the ink cassette.
08_crown                 marquee on top of the tower.
09_title_sign            your title art, 4 colours. Press or glue into the crown's front recess.
10_rear_paper_tunnel     optional. Sits behind the booth so paper can move in and out of the printer's back.

SETTINGS
PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill. Only 04 needs supports.

HARDWARE
Screws: M3 x 8 self-tapping (or M3 machine screws) x 16 for the case,
        M3 x 10 x 4 for the screen bars, M2.5 x 6 x 4 for the Pi, M2 x 5 x 4 for the camera.
1 mm foam tape between the screen and the retainer bars.

ASSEMBLY ORDER
1. Bolt 04 to 05 (tongues slide inside 05, 4 side screws).
2. Drop the SELPHY into the bay, then slide its paper tray in through the photo slot. The finished print lands on the tray, about 47 mm of it outside the booth.
3. Screw the camera onto the 4 posts behind the small square hole, lens through the hole.
4. Lay the tower face down. Set the touchscreen into the window, put 1 mm foam tape on its back, then screw the 2 bars over it.
5. Screw the crown on from inside the tower (2 screws up through the tower top, short screwdriver).
6. Screw the Pi onto 02. Connect camera ribbon, HDMI and touch USB. Run the printer USB and Pi power cable down through the slot in the tower floor (back left). Then screw 02 onto the tower.
7. Set the tower onto the base (its side skirts drop inside the base walls) and screw through the base sides.
8. Glue or double-sided tape the sign into the crown recess. Put on the lid and press in the ink cover.
9. The paper tunnel just sits behind the base. Power cords exit the open back.
